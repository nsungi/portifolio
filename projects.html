Contract Metadata Extraction System

Project Overview

This project focused on developing an advanced system to extract, process, and analyze metadata from business contracts. Working with international clients, the system automated the extraction of critical information from legal documents.

Challenges

  • Processing diverse formats of legal documents with varying structures
  • Ensuring high accuracy in metadata extraction from complex legal language
  • Integrating with existing business operations systems

Solutions

Leveraged cutting-edge technologies including Natural Language Processing (NLP), Large Language Models (LLM), and custom AI algorithms to analyze and extract relevant information. Implemented PostgreSQL for efficient data storage and retrieval.

Results

  • Reduced contract processing time by 75%
  • Improved metadata extraction accuracy to over 95%
  • Streamlined business operations through automated data extraction
